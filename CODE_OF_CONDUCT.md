# Code of conduct

## Our community

Relay Warden welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities and
levels of experience. Treat others with respect in issues, pull requests,
reviews and other project spaces. This policy also applies when someone
officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Make room for newcomers and differing perspectives.
- Respect people's privacy, boundaries and decisions not to engage.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their effects.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation and sharing someone's private information without
permission are not acceptable. Neither is repeated disruption after a
maintainer has asked for it to stop. Disagreement is welcome; abuse is not.

## Reporting an incident

Report concerns privately to [support@sudosy.fr](mailto:support@sudosy.fr)
with the subject **Relay Warden conduct report**. Do not post incident
details or personal information in a public issue. GitHub vulnerability
reports are for software vulnerabilities, not conduct incidents.

Include what happened, where it happened, relevant links or evidence, and
any immediate safety concerns. You do not need to confront the person
involved or resolve the incident yourself.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and limit sharing to what is
needed to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed, particularly where safety or legal obligations require action.
A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome; another
uninvolved maintainer should handle it where available. The project does
not currently have an independent appeals body.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request a correction, issue a
warning, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently exclude someone.
The response should reflect the seriousness, context and repetition of the
behavior; serious threats or harassment can warrant immediate exclusion.

Where safe and appropriate, maintainers will explain the decision privately
to those affected. A person may request reconsideration through the same
private reporting channel and provide new information. Retaliation against
someone who reports a concern or participates in a review is itself a
violation of this policy.
